Mission Briefing

Prompt Wars is a high-stress simulation designed to test your ability to command and manipulate AI systems using raw text constraints.

You will be locked into 5 sequential containment sectors. To progress, you must decipher the required logic and generate the exact target code.

WARNING: HIDDEN DIRECTIVEOperatives who clear all 5 sectors before life support failure will unlock the classified.

🎯 Primary Objective

Execute all overrides with maximum precision and speed before the master countdown hits zero.

🏆 Final Outcome

Your neural efficiency will be recorded on the Global Operative Registry.

⚠️ Operation Parameters

Review simulation rules before initializing terminal uplink.

⏱️ Life Support Timer

  • Facility allows 20 minutes total for all sectors.
  • Timer activates upon terminal initialization.
  • Countdown cannot be paused.
  • Depletion results in immediate simulation failure.

🔁 Security Lockouts

  • Rounds 1-3 have unrestricted attempts within the master timer.
  • Round 4 allows 3 attempts, round 5 allows 2 attempts, and the bonus round allows 1 submission.
  • Exhausting the capped rounds ends the run immediately.

🚫 Anti-Cheat Protocols

  • Focus loss, including tab switching, is strictly prohibited.
  • Violations deduct 15 seconds from life support.
  • Copy/Paste functions are disabled on standard terminals.
  • 3 violations results in disqualification.

🔒 Persistence Rules

  • Enter your real full name and approved @calfus.com email to unlock the terminal.
  • One attempt per operative identity.
  • Terminal reboots and refreshes will not reset the timer.
  • Connections resume from point of failure.

🏆 Evaluation Matrix Ranking Order

1. Rounds Reached
2. Accuracy + Speed Composite
3. Fewer Attempts / Round
4. Deterministic Tie-Break
